## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, technical, and dependable atmosphere tailored for higher education analytics, procurement intelligence, and artificial intelligence modeling. It targets academic administrators, financial directors, procurement analysts, and data science teams who require clarity over clutter and density without cognitive exhaustion.

The emotional signature is authoritative, sharp, and forward-looking. The visual language blends Modern Corporate SaaS with high-density Data Tooling:
- **Structural Integrity**: Anchored by deep, high-contrast navigation rails that frame bright, airy, hyper-clean analytical canvases.
- **Architectural Clarity**: Micro-deliberate typographic contrast, hairline divider precision, and controlled functional color mapping ensure data tables, metric tiles, and predictive pipelines remain immediately scannable.
- **Controlled Sophistication**: Eliminates ornamental skeuomorphism in favor of tonal grouping, balanced white space, and purposeful elevation tiers.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast, functional semantic signaling, and low eye fatigue during prolonged operational workflows.

### Primary & Action
- **Primary Action (`#2563EB`)**: Applied strictly to key focal points, interactive CTAs, active pagination steps, and high-priority filters.
- **Primary Hover/Active (`#1D4ED8`)**: Used for focused states, table selection checkboxes, and pressed interactive triggers.
- **Primary Subdued (`#EFF6FF`)**: Background fill for active items, selected navigation entries, and subtle contextual highlights.

### Structural Neutrals & Backgrounds
- **App Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: Ultra-soft neutral preventing glare on large data dashboards while preserving contrast against cards.
- **Surface Elevation Base (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for data cards, modals, table surfaces, and flyout drawers.
- **Structural Chrome / Deep Anchor (`#0F172A`)**: Applied to the primary vertical navigation sidebar and enterprise header elements to ground the UI.
- **Structural Chrome Muted (`#1E293B`)**: Hover states, active markers, and nested group backgrounds within dark structural zones.
- **Border Subtle (`#F1F5F9`)**: Internal table dividers and horizontal separator lines.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`)**: Outer bounding boxes of analytical cards, inputs, and tab containers.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Primary metrics, table values, column headers, and display titles.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`)**: Supporting descriptive copy, breadcrumbs, and helper annotations.
- **Text Tertiary / Placeholder (`#94A3B8`)**: Input placeholders, disabled indicators, and unit markers.

### Domain-Specific Semantic Accents
- **Machine Learning & AI Accent (`#6366F1`)**: Exclusively reserved for AI-generated recommendations, predictive models, score tags, and confidence interval curves. Light companion fill: `#EEF2FF`.
- **Positive & Trend Success (`#10B981`)**: KPI increments, positive budgetary margins, compliant items, and optimal health status. Light companion fill: `#ECFDF5`.
- **Warning & Attention (`#F59E0B`)**: Spend anomalies, approval bottlenecks, risk warnings, and medium-severity notices. Light companion fill: `#FFFBEB`.
- **Destructive & Error (`#EF4444`)**: Critical failures, procurement policy violations, and unrecoverable errors. Light companion fill: `#FEF2F2`.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes technical readability and data alignment:
- **Font Character**: Plus Jakarta Sans provides crisp legibility at small sizes while offering geometric distinction across headers and numeric readouts.
- **Tabular Numerics**: Metric readouts, data tables, and financial tallies must consistently utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact columnar alignment.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Headers use tight tracking (-0.01em to -0.025em) to prevent loose spacing in large metric callouts. Small analytical labels and badges employ slight positive tracking (+0.01em to +0.03em) to ensure high glanceability on dense dashboards.
- **Monospace Pairing**: JetBrains Mono is designated for algorithm IDs, query syntaxes, batch identifiers, and code parameters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 8pt grid system (with 4pt sub-steps for dense controls).

### Layout Philosophy & Shell
- **Structure**: Asymmetric application layout composed of a fixed-width dark navigation sidebar (`260px` expanded, `72px` compact icon rail) and a fluid analytical viewport.
- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column fluid grid system across tablet and desktop views. Content regions enforce a minimum width of `1024px` for complex multi-series charts and multi-column comparison tables, triggering clean horizontal scrolls on inner containers rather than crushing tabular metrics.
- **Canvas Boundaries**: Main dashboard canvas uses `margin: 2rem` (`32px`) padding on desktop, scaling to `1rem` (`16px`) on mobile devices.
- **Gutter Rules**: Standard data card grids utilize `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`). Dense configuration tables and filter toolbars drop to `gutter-sm: 1rem` (`16px`).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Strategy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: Persistent 260px sidebar, 12-column grid, full KPI card rows (4 columns).
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (1024px - 1279px)**: Compact 72px icon sidebar, 12-column grid, KPI cards reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 1024px)**: Collapsible drawer navigation, single-column KPI stacks, filter controls transition into bottom-sheet modals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean white surface elevations set against the ultra-soft neutral background (`#F8FAFC`), combined with crisp perimeter borders and tinted ambient drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Hex `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero shadow. Hosts workspace layouts and underlying grid foundations.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers)**: Hex `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for analytics cards, table wrappers, and metric panels.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover)**: Hex `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Used for hovered cards, active toolbar dropdown triggers, and contextual action menus.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts & Dropdowns)**: Hex `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Used for multi-select filter menus, date-range pickers, and popovers.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays)**: Hex `#FFFFFF`, shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a structural backdrop overlay: `#0F172A` at 60% opacity with a `4px` blur filter.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision with balanced rounding (`level 2` baseline) to maintain an enterprise data-tool aesthetic while softening high visual density:
- **Base Rounding (`rounded-md` / 8px)**: Applied to interactive form elements, text inputs, small buttons, status tags, and inline badges.
- **Large Rounding (`rounded-lg` / 16px)**: Applied to analytics cards, table containers, slide-over panels, and popover dialogs.
- **Extra Large Rounding (`rounded-xl` / 24px)**: Applied to high-level structural containers, primary hero dashboard widgets, and large modal windows.
- **Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Restricted entirely to status indicator dots, small count badges, and avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md` (8px). Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E40AF`. Focus: 2px ring offset with `#93C5FD`. Height: 36px (compact) or 40px (default). Typography: `label-lg`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, `#475569` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text.
- **AI Action Variant**: Solid `#6366F1` background or `#EEF2FF` tint with `#6366F1` text and an inline spark/intelligence icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: 38px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, 8px corner radius, `body-md` typography. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to `#2563EB`, paired with a 3px soft focus ring: `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Validation**: Error state applies 1px solid `#EF4444` and red focus ring; validation copy set in `body-sm` `#EF4444`.

### Cards & KPI Tiles
- **Structure**: `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg` (16px), 20px internal padding.
- **KPI Layout**: Top row contains the metric label (`label-md` in `#475569`) and an optional semantic badge. Center features the high-impact value (`metric-xl` in `#0F172A`). Bottom contains the trend delta pill (`+12.4%` in `#10B981` on `#ECFDF5`) paired with comparative baseline text (`vs. previous cycle`).

### Data Tables
- **Container**: Border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg` (16px), overflow hidden.
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, 40px height, 1px border bottom `#E2E8F0`. Text formatted in `label-sm` uppercase `#64748B`.
- **Rows**: Alternating hover fill `#F8FAFC`, active selection `#EFF6FF`. Height: 48px standard, 36px condensed data view. Border bottom: 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Numeric Cells**: Right-aligned, utilizing tabular numeric fonts.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Shape**: Compact `rounded-md` (6px) or pill `rounded-full`, 24px max height, padding: `2px 8px`. Typography: `label-sm`.
- **AI / ML Model Tag**: `#EEF2FF` background, `#6366F1` text, subtle `#C7D2FE` border.
- **Success / Optimal**: `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, `#A7F3D0` border.
- **Attention / Warning**: `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text, `#FDE68A` border.
- **Neutral / Draft**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, `#E2E8F0` border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: 16x16px, `rounded-sm` (4px), 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: `#2563EB` fill with a centered white check vector.
- **Radios**: 16x16px circle with a centered 6px solid dot on active selection.
